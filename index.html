Expert content review

You are rating candidate items for a new instrument, the Collective Political Agency scale (CPA). The scale measures an individually held perception of collective capacity: how the we looks from where each member stands. It is a state measure with a 3-month window, designed to move when organizing works.

Construct definitions

Your three tasks per item. (1) Relevance: how relevant is this item to the construct system as a whole? (2) Clarity: how clearly would a community member understand it? (3) Sorting: which construct does the item belong to? Items are presented in shuffled order without their intended labels; your independent sort is the test. A comment box is provided for suggested rewording. Item-level content validity (I-CVI) will be computed as the proportion of experts rating relevance 3 or 4; items below .78 will be revised or dropped (Polit & Beck). Sorting agreement assesses substantive validity (Anderson & Gerbing).
