Expert content review

Thank you for lending your time and expertise. You are rating candidate items for the Collective Political Agency scale (CPA), a state measure of how the us is experienced from one person's point of view, with a one-month reference window.

Three judgments per item.

  1. Relevance to CPA as a whole, not to any one component.
  2. Clarity of the wording itself: does the item carry a single plain reading, or is it ambiguous, double-barreled, or reliant on vocabulary a respondent might not share? (why we ask experts this)
  3. Sorting: the construct the item reads as expressing. Items appear unlabelled, in a random order. “None of these / unsure” is a valid answer.

Every item has a comment box, and a short section on content coverage follows the items. Methods and rationale covers why we are building this, how the ratings are scored, why the window is one month, and the citations.
